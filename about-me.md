Not really, I love change and doing different things. I love innovation and think i'm in a unique position with operating and learning AI tools.

Once I get to work I complete my safety documentation, get the loader started and im done that by about 8am. I get the semi and hook onto a trailer and bring it inside the tipping floor. I use the loader to start loading a trailer which takes about an hour and a half. Around 11 I have some time in between trailers so I check on my FORA progress while I eat lunch. Around 1pm I start loading another trailer. I typically deal with customer issues, logistical issues and equipment maintenance issues in between. I have another operator that works with me from 8-4. He runs the back hoe to compact the waste in the trailer. I finish my shift at 6pm, drive half an hour home. I eat dinner around 6:30pm, hang out with my daughter and wife untill my daughter goes to bed at 8. Then I come downstairs to my office and work on FORA.
