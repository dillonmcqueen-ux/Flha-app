Who built this

Built by the guy who had to fill it out.

Fifteen years in heavy civil. Water, sewer, infrastructure, and everything in between. Five years building digital solutions and testing ideas on the side, because the tools I needed either did not exist or were priced for somebody else.

I worked for small and mid-sized companies my whole career. The kind that know exactly what is broken and cannot justify twenty grand a year to fix it. Every piece of good software in this industry is built for the large general contractor with a safety department and a budget line for it. Everyone else gets a binder.

So I built it from the ground up, from the operator's point of view. Not from a product manager's whiteboard, and not from a demo of somebody else's software.

That means it has to work for the guy who still keeps his passwords on a piece of paper in his truck, on a phone, in the cold, with gloves on, and it has to work for the CEO, the safety coordinator and the operations manager on the same day. Those are four completely different people with four completely different problems, and most software in this space only ever solves for one of them.

I am still on a machine. I still deal with the paperwork, the logistics and the equipment issues that FORA exists to take off somebody's plate. That is not a marketing angle, it is just where the ideas come from.

No suits. No bs. Just results.

FORA is an insured, registered business in Central Alberta. There is no support queue and no account manager. You email the person who writes the code, and he emails you back.
