What is FORA

Paperwork that already knows your company.

FORA comes standard with five document types — Field Level Hazard Assessments, Toolbox Talks, Incident Reports, Near Miss Reports, and Equipment Inspections. Every one is generated with AI, and every one is shaped around your company, not a generic template.

Describe what happened, or what you're about to do, by voice or text. FORA cross-references it against your own SOPs, your own equipment, and your own site — then hands back a complete, compliant document in seconds.

Need something the standard five don't cover? That's what Custom Forms and Custom Builds are for. FORA is built around your company — not the other way around.

Standard, included with every plan

Five documents. One AI, tuned to your company.

Each of the five works differently, because each one asks something different of the person filling it out. Here's how AI handles each one.

🦺

FLHA

Describe the task by voice or text. FORA cross-references it against your company's own SOPs and hands back hazards, controls, required PPE, and compliance alerts.

🗣️

Toolbox Talk

Pick a topic and FORA drafts a complete 5–10 minute talk — ready for a supervisor to walk the crew through, tailored to your company and the work at hand.

🚨

Incident Report

Give it the raw details of what happened. FORA turns it into a formal, professional incident report — structured, complete, and ready to file.

👀

Near Miss Report

A quick description of what almost happened becomes a clean, professional near-miss report — nothing gets skipped because it felt too small to write up.

🔧

Equipment Inspection

Pick the machine. FORA generates a pre-use inspection checklist built for that exact type of equipment — a skid steer and a boom lift don't get the same checklist.

Supervisor Dashboard

Everything your crew files, in one screen.

Every FLHA, toolbox talk, incident, near miss, and equipment inspection your crew submits lands in the same place — grouped, filterable, and built for a supervisor scanning a busy week, not hunting through folders or a group chat.

Group by document type, site, or crew. Collapse what's already been reviewed. Flag anything that needs a second look. It's the same dashboard whether you're running a 6-person crew or a 40-person operation.

How your data is protected: every request is checked against a signed, server-verified session before it ever reaches your data — nothing gets through unverified. Direct database access is refused by default: Row Level Security is switched on for every table with no bypass policies, so the access-controlled dashboard is the only door in. And it's scoped per company, so your dashboard only ever shows your data, walled off from every other company on FORA.
