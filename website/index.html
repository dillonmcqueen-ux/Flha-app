What is FORA

Field safety paperwork, generated — not filled in.

Every shift starts with the same question: what are today's hazards? Normally that means digging through a binder, guessing at PPE, or copy-pasting last week's form.

FORA replaces the guesswork. A worker says or types what they're about to do, and FORA reads it against your company's own uploaded SOPs — not a generic template library — to produce hazards, controls, required PPE, and compliance alerts in seconds.

It's not a form builder wearing an AI badge. It's a safety platform that starts every document with your actual procedures.
