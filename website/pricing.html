    We don't do enterprise solutions.

    The entire idea behind FORA is one platform that does all of it at a price a small company can actually carry. Multiple subscriptions are what kill small businesses: four vendors, four renewal dates, four price increases a year, and none of them talk to each other. FORA is built to replace that stack, not become the fifth thing in it. There is no enterprise tier, no seat minimum, and no number on this page that changes because of who is asking.
    Cancel any time

    Built for companies that shut down for the winter.

    Seasonal work is normal in this industry. Being punished for it should not be.

    No contract, no term

    Cancel whenever you want. There is no minimum commitment, no annual lock-in and no cancellation fee.

    Pause for the season

    Shut down in November, come back in April. Your account is waiting where you left it.

    No second setup fee

    Email us to restart a paused account and you pick up the monthly price again, not the setup fee. You only ever pay that once. Do not use the checkout link to come back, that one bundles the setup fee in.

    Your data isn't hostage

    Your records stay yours. Export everything as PDFs any time your account is running, and we will get you your records on the way out too.
