See it work

Pick a task. Watch it write the FLHA.

These are pre-built examples so you can see the format and depth of a real FLHA without an account. On your own account it also cross-references your company's SOPs, which is the part that is different for every company.

The field side

What a worker sees on their phone.

One menu, grouped into Safety, Equipment and General. No training session. Most workers file their first document without anyone showing them how.

Field Level Hazard Assessment

Under a minute

"Digging a trench near the east fence line" is a complete input. That one sentence is enough to produce a full, signed FLHA.

  • Voice or text entry, no form to tab through
  • Cross-referenced against your own SOPs and equipment list
  • Returns hazards, controls, required PPE and risk ratings
  • Signed in the field, exported as a clean PDF
  • Autosaves as a draft so a dropped call does not lose it

Toolbox Talk

Ready to present

Pick a topic and get a 5 to 10 minute talk a supervisor can walk straight into with the crew, no prep.

  • Written around your operations, not a stock topic off a list
  • Tied to the work actually happening that day
  • Attendance captured with the record
  • Filed and exportable like every other document

Incident Report

Regulator ready

Give it the raw details in whatever order they come out. It comes back as a formal, structured report.

  • Photo attachments stored against the report
  • Structured enough to hand to a regulator or insurer
  • Nothing gets lost because the person writing it was rattled

Near Miss Report

Catches patterns

A quick description of what almost happened becomes a clean report in well under a minute.

  • Low enough friction that the small ones actually get filed
  • The pattern shows up before it becomes an incident
  • Rolls into safety analytics automatically

Equipment Inspection

Per machine type

Pick the machine and get the pre-use checklist built for that exact type of equipment.

  • Pre-trip and post-trip both supported
  • A skid steer and a boom lift get different checklists
  • Defects flag straight to the supervisor dashboard
  • Feeds the maintenance view, so a flagged machine does not get forgotten

Time Clock

GPS on every punch

Clock in and out against the company roster, with the location of the punch recorded.

  • GPS coordinates captured at clock-in and clock-out
  • Punches plotted on a map in the dashboard
  • Hours roll into a time clock report you can export
  • Replaces the paper sign-in sheet and the separate app nobody checks

Certification Tracking

Alerts before expiry

Every ticket and certificate your crew holds, in one directory, with a warning before one lapses.

  • Workers add and view their own tickets
  • Expired and expiring-soon counts surface on the worker's menu and the dashboard
  • Company-wide directory with photos, so a supervisor can check in seconds

Fuel & Consumables Log

Adds up at month end

The clipboard in the truck, turned into a log tied to the actual machine.

  • Logged against a specific unit, not a scrap of paper
  • Rolls into equipment analytics
  • Works offline and submits when signal comes back

Daily Report

End of shift

An end-of-day site summary filed by the person who was actually there.

  • Site, crew, work completed, conditions
  • Groups by site and crew on the dashboard
  • Exports as a PDF you can send straight to a client

Monthly Site Inspection

Your checklist

A recurring compliance walk-through built from your own checklist, not a generic industry template.

  • Checklist built to your wording and changed whenever you ask, no dev work
  • Corrective actions tracked separately until they are closed
  • Month-over-month record for an audit

The Brain

Why your FLHAs get better and a competitor's don't.

Every FORA account gets its own Brain the day it is set up. Not a chatbot. Not a general-purpose model with your logo on it. A memory built entirely out of your own documentation.

01

It starts from your SOPs, not the internet

At onboarding it reads your SOPs and equipment list and builds a first-pass profile: your industry, your terminology, your gear, your sites. No web research on your company or your industry, ever. Just what you sent, however messy it was when it arrived.

02

It learns from real corrections, not every click

When a supervisor substantively fixes something an AI-generated document got wrong or left out, that is a signal worth learning from. Rubber-stamped, unedited output is not, and does not count.

03

It sits underneath every feature, not inside one

Terminology and hazard emphasis picked up from an FLHA edit is available to a toolbox talk, an incident report and every custom build in the same account. The document types are not separate tools that happen to share a login.

04

It can only ever raise the bar

This one is structural, not a prompt instruction. The Brain can shift emphasis and terminology, but it can never soften a risk rating or skip a required hazard category. Safety-critical output never gets less cautious because of usage history.

05

You can read exactly what it learned

Nothing about it is a black box. Ask any time and you get a PDF snapshot of your company's Brain profile: industry, terminology, equipment, and what it has picked up from real usage.

Underneath all of it

Works without signal

Submissions queue on the phone and go up when service comes back. A dead zone does not cost you a document.

A half-finished form survives a locked screen, a dropped call, or a worker getting pulled onto something else.

Unlimited PDF export

Every document type exports as a properly formatted, branded PDF. No export limits, no per-document charge.

Workers sign in with a PIN, not a corporate email nobody has. Forgot one? A supervisor resets it from the dashboard in seconds.

Custom forms

Analytics with exports

Safety and equipment analytics, both exportable as a PDF when a prime contractor or an insurer asks for numbers.

Punch map

Features you don't use, switched off

Any built-in document type can be switched off for your company on request. Your crew only sees what you actually use.

Security

Your data, walled off from every other company on FORA.

This is not a setting somebody has to remember to switch on. It is how the system is built.

LayerWhat it means
Signed sessionsEvery request is checked against a signed, server-verified session before it reaches your data. Nothing gets through unverified.
Deny by defaultRow Level Security is enabled on every table with no bypass policies, so direct database access is refused outright. The access-controlled dashboard is the only door in.
Company scopingEvery read and write is scoped to your company. Your dashboard can only ever return your data.
Private file storageDocuments and photos live in private storage and are served through time-limited signed links, never a public URL.
Your data stays yoursExport your records as PDFs whenever you want. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used to train anything outside your own account.
