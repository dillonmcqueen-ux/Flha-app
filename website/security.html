Security

Stored in Canada. Walled off from every other company.

Not a setting somebody has to remember to turn on. It's how the system is built, and it gets checked, not just claimed.

Ask a question See the platform
Checked, not just claimed

An automated audit runs every three days.

Most companies write a security page once and never look at it again. FORA's gets re-verified on a schedule: storage buckets, database access rules, secrets in the codebase, and the attacker's-eye view of the whole application. Anything it finds gets fixed or flagged, not filed away.

Every 3 days
Storage exposure, database access rules, hardcoded secrets, signed-URL discipline, external surfaces and an attacker's-mindset pass, all re-checked on a fixed schedule.
Canada
Your records and uploaded files are stored in a database hosted in Canada.
SOC 2
Our database provider holds a SOC 2 Type 2 report, audited by a third party.
Alberta PIPA
Personal information is handled under Alberta's PIPA and federal PIPEDA.
Access control

Deny by default. Your company only ever sees your company.

Multi-tenant isolation isn't a setting, it's the default every table starts from. Nothing is readable or writable until a rule explicitly says a signed-in user from your company can touch it.

Signed sessions

Every request is checked against a signed, server-verified session first.

Deny by default

Row Level Security on every table, no bypass policies. The dashboard is the only door in.

Company scoping

Every read and write is scoped to your company. You only ever see your data, never another company's.

Private file storage

Documents and photos are served through time-limited signed links, never a public URL.

Your data stays yours

Export any time. Nothing is sold or used to train anything outside your account.

Straight about the rest

Hosting and AI processing run through the providers named in our Privacy Policy.
