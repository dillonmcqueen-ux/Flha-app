How it works

Paperwork that already knows your company.

Describe what happened, or what you're about to do, by voice or text. FORA cross-references it against your own SOPs, your own equipment, and your own site — then hands back a complete, compliant document in seconds.

Nothing generic gets handed back. The hazards, the controls, the checklist items — all of it reflects your company's actual procedures, because that's what FORA was given to work from.

Every document lands on the Supervisor Dashboard, ready for sign-off, review, and export — right alongside anything built for you as a Custom Build.

The five, in full

One AI, tuned to your company, five different jobs.

Each of the five works differently, because each one asks something different of the person filling it out.

🦺

Field Level Hazard Assessment (FLHA)

Describe the task by voice or text — "digging a trench near the east fence line" is enough. FORA cross-references your company's own SOPs and equipment list, then hands back the hazards, controls, required PPE, and any compliance alerts specific to that task. Signed off in the field, stored with every other submission, exportable as a clean PDF.

🗣️

Toolbox Talk

Pick a topic and FORA drafts a complete 5–10 minute talk a supervisor can walk straight into with the crew — ready to go, tailored to your company's operations and the work actually happening that day, not a stock topic pulled off a list.

🚨

Incident Report

Give it the raw, unfiltered details of what happened, in whatever order they come out. FORA turns that into a formal, structured incident report — complete, professional, and ready to file, or to hand to a regulator or insurer if it ever comes to that.

👀

Near Miss Report

A quick description of what almost happened becomes a clean, professional near-miss report in under a minute — so the things that felt "too small to write up" actually get written up, and the pattern behind them gets caught before it becomes an incident.

🔧

Equipment Inspection

Pick the piece of equipment and FORA generates the pre-use inspection checklist built for that exact type — a skid steer and a boom lift don't get the same checklist, and neither should your crew's paperwork.
