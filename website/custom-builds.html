What's actually possible

If it's a process, it can probably be built.

The Big 5 cover what almost every crew files day to day. Custom Builds are for the parts of your operation that are specific to you — the spreadsheet nobody trusts anymore, the paper log that lives in a truck, the process you've been meaning to fix for two years. A Custom Build isn't bolted on beside FORA — it's built into it, using the same login, the same roster, and the same Supervisor Dashboard your crew already knows.

How it works

Four steps from idea to something you can log into.

01

Tell us what's broken

No spec doc required. Describe the process, the form, or the headache in plain language — a voice note, an email, or a call works fine.

02

We scope it

You get a plain-language breakdown of what would actually get built, and a fixed quote before any work starts — no hourly rate, no surprises.

03

It gets built into your account

Not a separate app, not a separate login — it shows up inside FORA, next to the Big 5, using the same roster and dashboard your crew already knows.

04

You only pay for what's specific to you

Auth, database, PDF exports, and multi-tenant security already exist. A Custom Build is the part that's actually new — which is why it costs less than people assume.

Always learning

Meet the Brain — not a chatbot, a memory.

Every FORA account has one. It's not something you talk to — it's what makes the Big 5 and every Custom Build get sharper and more specific to your company the longer your crew uses it.

01

It starts with your own SOPs, not the internet

When your company onboards, the Brain reads your SOPs and equipment list to build a first-pass profile — your industry, your terminology, your gear. No web research on your company or your industry, ever — just what you actually sent us.

02

It keeps learning from real usage

Every time a supervisor edits an AI-generated FLHA, runs a toolbox talk, or logs an incident or near miss, that's a signal. Rubber-stamped, unedited AI output doesn't count — only real, substantive corrections shape what FORA learns.

03

It can only ever raise the bar, never lower it

This is the one rule that isn't negotiable: the Brain can shift emphasis and terminology, but it can never soften a risk rating or skip a required hazard category — structurally, not just by prompt wording. Safety-critical output never gets less cautious because of usage history.

04

You can see exactly what it's learned

Nothing about it is a black box. Every company's profile — industry, terminology, what it's picked up on — is fully visible and editable from the admin panel, the same way your SOPs and equipment already are.

A Custom Build isn't just built once and left alone — it's built into the same account the Brain already knows, so it benefits from the same company-specific context as the Big 5 from day one.

Priced by project scope

More affordable than most people think.

Custom software has a reputation for six-figure price tags and six-month timelines. That's not how this works. A Custom Build isn't a platform built from scratch — it's a new piece added to a platform that already exists. The login system, the database, the PDF exports, the security, the mobile-friendly interface — all of that is already built and already running. You're only paying for the part that's actually specific to your company, which is usually a lot smaller than it sounds. Custom Builds are quoted by project scope — tell us what you need, and you'll get a real number, not a guess.

Quotes come directly from the person who'll build it — a real person, based in Central Alberta, not a sales team reading from a script.
