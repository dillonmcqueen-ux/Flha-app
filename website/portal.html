The process

Four steps from a box of paper to a login.

01

Tell us what you have

Email us the forms you use today, or a photo of the pile. A short call works too. Rough numbers are fine: how many people, how many forms.

02

You get a written quote

We go through your forms with you and send a priced proposal built around exactly what you described. Nothing gets built until you approve it.

03

We build it

Once you approve and the invoice is paid, you send the documents and we rebuild every one as a digital form. The building is our job, not yours.

04

Your crew logs in

They use it on a real site with a real crew. Anything that doesn't match how you run gets changed.

To get a quote

Four things to send us.

A few lines is enough to start. We ask for the rest on the call.

Your forms

A photo or scan of each form you use today, or the files if you already have them. Messy is fine.

Your people

Roughly how many people fill them out, and who they are: crew, supervisors, office.

Who needs to see what

Which department gets each one, such as safety, HR, payroll, maintenance or your operations manager.

How often

Daily, weekly, monthly or one off. It decides how each form is set up.

What's in your box?

Email a few lines about what you run on paper to quotes@forafieldsolutions.com. You get a straight answer on whether it fits, and a written quote, from the person who builds it.
