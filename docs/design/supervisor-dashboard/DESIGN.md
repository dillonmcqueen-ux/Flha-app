## Brand & Style

The design system projects high-velocity technical capability, engineering rigor, and forward-leaning operational focus. Built for modern field and industrial technology applications, the visual language merges high-contrast minimalism with sharp, utilitarian technical aesthetics.

The emotional tone balances absolute dependability with striking modern momentum. Layouts are disciplined, deliberate, and uncluttered, pairing stark black and white contrasts with razor-sharp electric international safety orange accents. Visual structures favor crisp micro-details, precision monospaced coordinates, strong editorial-technical headlines, and robust utility to convey authority in complex operational environments.

## Colors

The color palette centers on intense contrast and functional clarity. The default color mode is dark, engineered to evoke high-end field hardware interfaces, command dashboards, and technical precision instruments.

- **Primary (`#FF6B00`)**: A vivid, high-visibility kinetic orange. Used selectively for critical call-to-actions, active telemetry points, operational alerts, and focused accentuating strokes.
- **Neutral (`#000000`)**: Deep void black serving as the core backdrop across main canvases.
- **Surfaces & Layers**: Elevated surfaces graduate up through `#0F0F0F` (cards, containers) and `#181818` (flyouts, modal overlays), structured by razor-thin dark neutral borders (`#282828`).
- **Typography & Highlighting**: Stark white (`#FFFFFF`) guarantees effortless scanability for primary content, accompanied by calibrated mid-tone grays (`#A3A3A3`, `#666666`) to dictate secondary metadata hierarchy.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the geometric, technological architecture of **Space Grotesk** with the neutral, hyper-legible utility of **Inter**.

Space Grotesk handles displays, titles, data telemetry figures, buttons, and navigation badges. Its angular personality reinforces technical capability and structure. Inter governs running copy, descriptive documentation, forms, and general content flows, delivering clarity even under rapid scanning conditions.

All uppercase labels in Space Grotesk require generous tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em` to `0.06em`) to echo industrial instrumentation labeling. Mobile scales down titles cleanly below the 32px threshold to preserve punchiness while eliminating awkward wraps on constrained viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system implements an exact 12-column fluid grid system on desktop that translates into a 6-column grid on tablet and a 4-column structure on mobile devices.

### Breakpoints & Fluidity
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, `margin: 2rem` (capped at maximum container width 1440px), `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 6 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.
- **Mobile (< 767px)**: 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.

Component padding strictly observes the 8pt rhythm, using `space-2xs` (4px) and `space-xs` (8px) for compact toolbars and badges, progressing to `space-xl` (40px) and `space-2xl` (64px) for section delineations. Elements are assembled within structural zones defined by clear vertical axes and aligned baseline grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids decorative drop shadows, relying instead on **tactile tonal layering** and **low-contrast precision outlines**.

- **Tonal Tiering**: Layers elevate cleanly from base `#000000` to container `#0F0F0F`, up to floating overlays at `#181818`.
- **Precision Framing**: Containers, panels, and cards use 1px solid borders (`#282828`). On hover or active state, the border highlights into subtle orange tinting or crisp high-contrast white boundaries.
- **Accented Focus**: When an element demands direct elevation (such as a modal or active pinned HUD element), it uses an ambient, highly diffused perimeter glow: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.15)`.
- **Inner Rim Lighting**: Critical callouts and cards can deploy a subtle inset top edge `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to simulate machined physical hardware bevels.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an engineering-inspired shape profile (`roundedness: 1`).

Standard interactive modules, buttons, form controls, and cards employ a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, preserving a rigid, architectural feel. Secondary cards and surface groupings scale up to `0.5rem` (8px). Pills and circular shapes are avoided except for status indication pips and live telemetry badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#FF6B00` fill, `#000000` bold text in Space Grotesk (`label-md`), 4px radius. Padding: 12px 24px. Hover transforms background to `#E55E00` with subtle upward 1px translation.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Transparent background, 1px `#282828` border, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts border color to `#FFFFFF` and background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background, `#A3A3A3` text. Hover brightens text to `#FFFFFF` with no border shift.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Dark container fill (`#0F0F0F`), 1px solid border (`#282828`), 4px radius. Text in Inter (`body-md`, `#FFFFFF`), placeholder in `#666666`.
- **Active / Focus**: Border shifts instantly to `#FF6B00` with an outer outline ring of `2px solid rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.2)`. No rounded inner shadows.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 18px box with 1px border (`#282828`). Checked state fills `#FF6B00` featuring a solid `#000000` glyph check or core pip.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips**: Background `rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.08)`, 1px border `rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.3)`, text `#FF6B00` (`label-sm`), uppercase with tracking `0.05em`.
- **Neutral Badges**: Background `#181818`, 1px border `#282828`, text `#A3A3A3`.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from `#0F0F0F` with a 1px `#282828` perimeter border and 4px or 8px radius.
- Internal padding adheres to `space-lg` (24px) for desktop, reducing to `space-md` (16px) on mobile.
- Interactive cards incorporate a subtle border transition to `#404040` on hover, with header titles set in Space Grotesk (`headline-sm`).

### Lists & Data Displays
- Border-separated row items with 1px dividers (`#1A1A1A`).
- Monospaced metric tags, high-contrast labels, and clear chevron indicators for navigable rows. Mobile lists retain 48px minimum touch targets for high-stress field usability.
