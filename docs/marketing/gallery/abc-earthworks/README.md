# ABC Earthworks screenshot gallery

Open `index.html` in a browser. Every image has a Download button.

TL;DR: real screens from the live app, real PDFs the app generated, and framed mockups. All of it is ABC Earthworks (company code `ABCEARTH`), the test only company. None of it is factual. Never quote it as customer data in marketing, case studies, proposals or reports.

## What is in here

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Owner dashboard | Every tab of the owner dashboard at 1440px, plus the record detail pop ups |
| Roster and company setup | Departments, divisions, sites by division, document assignments, notification rules |
| Supervisor view | A Civil division supervisor, so counts are scoped to their division |
| Worker screens (7 sections) | Phone size walkthroughs of FLHA, equipment inspection, toolbox talk, near miss, incident, daily report, fuel, service, monthly inspection and a custom form |
| Login and company setup | Company code, name and PIN screens, and the new company intake form |
| PDF outputs | Real PDFs generated by the app from real submissions, page by page |
| Marketing mockups | Phone and browser frames on the FORA dark ground, plus a hero shot |

Folders: `images/` holds full size WebP files, `thumbs/` holds the previews, `manifest.json` lists every image with its title.

## The made up company

- ABC Earthworks Company, central Alberta civil and utilities contractor
- 21 people: an owner, 4 supervisors, 15 workers, plus the real owner login that already existed on the test company
- 8 sites across Civil, Utilities and Yard divisions, 17 machines, 12 weeks of history (about 750 inspections, 180 FLHAs, 13 toolbox talks, 8 near misses, 5 incidents, 84 daily reports, 280 fuel logs, 1,000 time clock punches, 19 monthly inspections, 64 custom form records)
- Deliberate problem cases so the alerts have something to show: expired and expiring tickets, an overdue CVIP, two overdue and two due soon maintenance items, open corrective actions, an incident waiting for review

## Things to know before you reuse an image

- The roster pickers on a few phone screens list "Dillon McQueen" and "Marcus Delaney". Dillon is the real owner login on the test company. Crop or blur it for anything public.
- The AI written text in the real submissions (FLHA, toolbox talk, near miss, incident, daily report, monthly summary, custom form) was generated live by the app, so it is genuine product output.
- Signatures on seeded records are drawn placeholders. The ones on the PDFs were drawn during the captures.
- Lists show "No PDF" on seeded records because only the records submitted through the app during the captures have real PDF files.
- Dashboard dates read Wednesday, October 7, 2026. Re-shoot if the date matters.
- The ABC logo is the placeholder DEMO hexagon already on the test company.
- No Admin Panel screens. That panel is founder only, so the form builders, Brain tab and platform views are not here.

## One thing found while shooting

Signing a near miss, an incident, or anything saved to sign afterwards fails in Chromium based browsers. `src/NearMiss.jsx:31`, `src/Incident.jsx:101` and `src/SignAfterwards.jsx:165` and `:295` read the signature with `fetch(dataUrl)`, and the Content Security Policy in `vercel.json` has `connect-src 'self' https://wzyvbtzxxdcxgvbkcqmt.supabase.co` with no `data:`. The browser blocks the fetch and the worker sees "Couldn't save your signature". The signing screens in this gallery were captured with CSP bypassed for that reason. This has not been fixed.
