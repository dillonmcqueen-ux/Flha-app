## Pain points discovered

- **Implementation/setup friction** is a recurring 2026 buyer-guide theme — legacy systems described as taking "weeks to implement," plus sync issues and learning-curve complaints once live; standard advice is to pilot with 5-10 users for 2-4 weeks before wider rollout. Sharpens baseline pain point #17 (generic tools require configuration) — and reinforces FORA's existing no-sales-funnel, live-in-minutes positioning (`docs/competitive-notes.md`) as a real answer to a real, named pain point, not just a nice-to-have. (#17, sharpened) ([source](https://www.corfix.com/blog/best-construction-safety-software-for-contractors-in-2026/))

## Mid-market opportunity notes

Nothing new and segment-specific this week beyond the implementation-friction point above, which hits smaller contractors hardest since they lack dedicated IT/rollout staff to absorb a multi-week implementation. Company Brain is also relevant here: it's a compounding-value feature ("gets better the longer you use it") that costs the customer nothing extra to benefit from — a good, concrete counter to Mitti's "you pay for capability you don't use" breadth problem called out in the baseline analysis.
