## Pain points discovered

- **SiteDocs renewal "bait and switch," confirmed and specific**: a named customer paid ~$3,700 CDN in year one, then saw the renewal price surge to ~$4,989 via a forced tier change (28–40% increase depending on discount); even downgrading cost 21% more than year one for half the functionality. Direct quote: "avoid them unless you want to spend months setting up and then get gouged at renewal." This is a much sharper, more usable version of the general per-seat-pricing complaint from prior weeks. (sharpens #16) ([source](https://softwarefinder.com/accounting-software/sitedocs/reviews))
- Enterprise EHS platforms at the heavier end confirmed to carry $4,000+ floor prices, $245–500/tech/month, $5K–50K mandatory implementation, and 2–3 year minimum contracts — explicitly "not optimized for ≤3 technicians." Confirms #16 at the extreme end (a heavier tier than the baseline's named eight competitors). ([source](https://www.makesafetyeasy.com/blog/construction-safety-software-pricing-2026))

## Mid-market opportunity notes

Two adjacent-vertical competitors surfaced this week worth knowing about, not worrying about: **BasinCheck**, an oil & gas-focused safety platform explicitly marketed as a "SafetyCulture alternative" for the 20–300-employee segment enterprise EHS platforms ignore, flat-priced $149–599/month regardless of crew size; and **Site Safety AI**, a $29.99/month single-feature tool doing AI photo hazard scanning with severity ratings. Neither competes with FORA directly (different vertical, narrower scope), but both validate the same bet FORA is making — flat pricing plus a tightly-scoped focus beats general EHS breadth for this buyer segment. Site Safety AI in particular is worth a direct look: it's already shipped a cheap version of an idea flagged in this pipeline two weeks ago.
