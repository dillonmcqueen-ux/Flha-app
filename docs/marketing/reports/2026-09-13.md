## New tech worth adopting

**Hybrid AI routing** is the concrete 2026 pattern, not just "costs are falling": send high-volume, easy requests (structured extraction, classification, form-field parsing) to a cheap open-weight model ($0.04–$0.15/million input tokens), reserve a stronger model for the genuinely hard requests (actual hazard-analysis generation). Quality loss on the easy bucket is reportedly small (~2%) even with big cost drops (~56%). *Effort: a routing change, not a rebuild* — this makes the standing "audit AI calls" item concrete: split FORA's AI calls into easy/hard buckets and move the easy ones first.
